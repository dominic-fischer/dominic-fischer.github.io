Computational linguist with a BA and an MSc in Computational Linguistics and Language Technology from the University of Zurich (MSc GPA 5.7/6), with a minor in Comparative Romance Linguistics.

Research assistant at the Department of Computational Linguistics, UZH (2023–2026), in the projects Bullinger Digital and Machine Translation for Romansh Idioms. Evaluated LLM-based translation of historical texts and developed a dictionary-based lemmatizer for Romansh, released as a Python package. Six published papers across the two projects.

Further academic activities: tutor for introductory computational linguistics courses and Database Systems (2022–2024), and president of the UZH Computational Linguistics student association (2024–2026).

Masters thesis: training byte-level language models, modeled on Meta AIs Byte Latent Transformer (BLT), and evaluating their fairness across languages.

Technical experience: Python, PyTorch, and Hugging Face; LLMs (APIs, evaluation, RAG, MCP); model training and evaluation; REST APIs and full-stack development (Flask, React, Streamlit); and AI-assisted development.

Languages: fluent in German, English, French, and Romanian, with strong proficiency in Italian, Spanish, and Portuguese.
