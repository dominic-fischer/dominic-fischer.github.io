*This project was done as part of the course 'Language Technology and Web Applications' in the autumn semester of 2023*

**A Sky of Word Clouds** is a fun and educational word-guessing game that introduces players to linguistic diversity through visually engaging word clouds. In the game, you're presented with multiple word clouds with words in different languages — and your task is to **find the "impostor" language that doesn't belong with the others**, i.e., that is from another language family. It is available as a [browser application](https://fishm4n.pythonanywhere.com/word_clouds/). More information about the game can be found in the [repository](https://github.com/dominic-fischer/A-Sky-of-Word-Clouds).
