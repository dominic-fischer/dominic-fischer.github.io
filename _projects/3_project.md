A Sky of Word Clouds is a fun and educational word-guessing game that introduces players to linguistic diversity through visually engaging word clouds. In the game, you're presented with multiple word clouds generated from words in different languages — and your task is to find the "impostor" language that doesn't belong with the others. With different game modes and difficulty levels, it mixes visual pattern recognition with language learning. The project includes a playable demo, screenshots, and is built using a mix of Python, JavaScript, HTML, and CSS.
